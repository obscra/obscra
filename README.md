## ◢ What is OBSCRA?

**OBSCRA** is a decentralized, **ZK-verified data marketplace built on Solana**. Store, sell, and auction your data with cryptographic proofs, IPFS storage, and instant SOL settlement — all without ever handing your keys to a middleman.

> Trade without asking for permission. Encryption happens on your device, payments hit your wallet directly, and no one takes a cut of what's yours.
